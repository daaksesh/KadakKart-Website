ABOUT KADAKKART

EVERYDAY.
DONE KADAK.

KadakKart is an Indian everyday-wear brand being built around one belief: the clothes you wear most deserve the most thought.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

LESS NOISE.
MORE CHARACTER.

Comfort. Clothing should feel good for hours, not just look good for five minutes.

Substance. We care about composition, GSM, finish, construction and fit — the details behind the label.

Honesty. We will tell you what a material does well and where it has limitations. No “miracle fabric” language.

Everyday value. We want products people can reach for repeatedly, not pieces that exist only for a trend.

01

INDIAN IN MIND

We think about real Indian weather, routines and wardrobes — from hot afternoons to humid monsoons.

02

QUALITY YOU CAN FEEL

Our first tee uses 100% cotton, 180 GSM and a biowashed finish. We will keep explaining the “why” behind each choice.

03

BUILT TO EVOLVE

We start with a focused T-shirt collection and expand only when a new product earns its place.

WHAT KADAK MEANS

CONFIDENT.
DIRECT.
UNAPOLOGETIC.

“Kadak” is a word with attitude. It can mean strong, solid, excellent, impressive — and it has a distinctly Indian familiarity.

That is the personality we want the brand to carry: confident without being pretentious, expressive without being loud, and unmistakably rooted in where we are from.

Stay Kadak. Wear the basic. Own the attitude.
