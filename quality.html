THE FABRIC GUIDE

WHAT'S YOUR
TEE MADE OF?

There is more to a T-shirt than colour and fit. Fibre, construction and weight change how it feels in India's heat, humidity and everyday life.

01

100% COTTON

Naturally breathable and comfortable against the skin. Cotton absorbs perspiration well, making it a strong everyday choice. The trade-off: in very humid conditions, absorbed moisture can take longer to dry.

Best for: Everyday wear · Indian summers · Casual use
02

LINEN

Excellent airflow and fast drying make linen attractive in peak heat. But it wrinkles easily and its texture is less conventional for a daily T-shirt.

Best for: Peak summer · Relaxed resort dressing
03

POLYESTER

Strong, durable and fast-drying. Performance polyester can move sweat to the surface, making it useful for sport. For everyday casual wear, feel and breathability depend heavily on construction and finish.

Best for: Sports · Training · Performance wear
04

COTTON BLENDS

Cotton-poly and similar blends balance comfort, durability, wrinkle resistance and cost. The exact ratio matters: more synthetic content changes the hand feel and moisture behaviour.

Best for: Easy-care everyday basics · Uniforms
05

MODAL / VISCOSE

Soft, smooth and drapey regenerated-cellulose fibres. They can feel excellent in warm weather, but care, wet strength and drying behaviour vary by fibre and construction.

Best for: Soft premium basics · Relaxed fits
06

LINEN / COTTON

A useful middle ground: linen adds airflow and faster drying while cotton adds softness and everyday familiarity. The blend ratio changes the final character.

Best for: Hot-weather casual · Summer layering

OUR CHOICE

WHY 100% COTTON
FOR KADAKKART?

We are building KadakKart around the T-shirt people actually want to wear repeatedly. For everyday Indian conditions, 100% cotton gives us the familiar combination of breathability, softness and natural comfort we want at the skin.

Our first tee is 180 GSM and biowashed — a deliberate balance between a flimsy lightweight tee and a heavy streetwear construction.
