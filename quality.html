QUALITY, WITHOUT THE JARGON

WHAT MAKES A
GOOD TEE?

A T-shirt is not just a fibre name. GSM, knit construction, finish, fit and care all change the experience. We want KadakKart customers to understand the choices behind the label.

01

100% COTTON

Cotton is a natural fibre commonly used for everyday T-shirts. It absorbs moisture and can feel comfortable and breathable against the skin. In humid weather, however, absorbed moisture can take longer to evaporate.

Why we chose it: familiar comfort + everyday versatility.
02

180 GSM

GSM means grams per square metre and describes fabric weight. 180 GSM gives our first tee more substance than a very lightweight basic without moving into a deliberately heavy streetwear construction.

Why we chose it: a balanced everyday weight.
03

BIOWASHED

Biowashing is a finishing process used to improve the surface feel of cotton by reducing loose fibres and creating a smoother, softer hand. The exact result depends on the fabric and process.

Why we chose it: a softer, cleaner everyday feel.
04

ROUND-NECK FIT

The first KadakKart silhouette is intentionally uncomplicated: a round neck, everyday proportions and a shape designed to work on its own or under a layer.

Why we chose it: repeat wear over novelty.
05

CONSTRUCTION MATTERS

The same fibre can feel completely different depending on yarn, knit structure, finishing, colour and garment construction. That is why a fibre label alone cannot tell the whole story.

Our rule: look beyond the fibre percentage.
06

CARE MATTERS

How a tee is washed and dried affects longevity, shape and feel. Following the garment care label is part of getting the most out of the product.

Our rule: buy well, wear often, care properly.

THE KADAK CHOICE

MADE FOR
INDIAN EVERYDAY.

India is not one climate. Coastal cities can be hot and humid, inland areas can experience intense dry heat, and monsoon conditions bring a very different moisture challenge. There is therefore no single fabric that is perfect for every person, city or activity.

For our first everyday tee, we chose 100% cotton + 180 GSM + biowashed finish because it gives us the comfort, substance and familiar hand feel we want for repeated casual wear.
