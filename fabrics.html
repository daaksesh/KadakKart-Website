KNOW YOUR TEE

THE MATERIAL
CHEAT SHEET.

There is no magic fibre. Different materials solve different problems. Here is a practical guide to the common fibres and blends you will encounter in T-shirts.

MaterialTypical feelMoisture / dryingWhere it shinesWatch-out
100% CottonSoft, familiar, breathableAbsorbs moisture; slower to dry when saturatedEveryday casual wearCan feel damp in high humidity
LinenAiry, textured, crispGood airflow; can dry relatively quicklyHot-weather dressingWrinkles easily; less common in classic tees
PolyesterVaries by knit and finishLow absorbency; performance knits can dry quickly and wickSports and activewearSome constructions can feel synthetic or warm
Cotton-PolyesterBalancedDepends on blend ratioEasy-care basics, uniformsMore polyester changes hand feel and moisture behaviour
ModalVery soft, smooth, drapeyAbsorbent; drying behaviour depends on constructionSoft premium casualwearLess structured feel; care varies
Viscose / RayonSoft, fluid, cool-feelingAbsorbent; wet strength and drying varySoft fashion topsCan need more careful handling
Lyocell / Tencel™Soft, smooth, fluidGood moisture management; construction mattersPremium casual and relaxed fitsHigher-cost option; not identical to every other cellulose fibre
Bamboo ViscoseSoft, smoothAbsorbency depends on constructionSoft casual basicsOften marketed simply as “bamboo”; fibre processing matters
HempDry, textured, increasingly soft with wearBreathable; moisture behaviour varies by blendNatural-fibre casualwearCan feel more textured and less conventional
Cotton-LinenAiry, soft, texturedGood airflow; blend ratio mattersHot-weather casualMore creasing and texture
NylonSmooth, strong, lightweightLow absorbency; dries quicklyPerformance / outdoor garmentsLess natural hand feel for basic casual tees

CHOOSE FOR THE
WEATHER. NOT THE HYPE.

Hot + dry: airflow and lightweight construction matter. Hot + humid: comfort, airflow and how the fabric handles absorbed moisture become important. Monsoon: faster-drying constructions can be useful. Sport: performance polyester or nylon can make more sense than a traditional cotton tee.

For an everyday casual T-shirt, KadakKart chooses 100% cotton at 180 GSM with a biowashed finish — a deliberate middle ground between a flimsy lightweight tee and a heavy, warm construction.

Basic. But never boring.
