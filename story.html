THE IDEA

BASIC ISN'T
A BAD WORD.

KadakKart begins with the most ordinary item in a wardrobe: the round-neck T-shirt.

We believe an everyday basic deserves more thought — better fabric, considered weight, a comfortable finish and a fit that earns repeat wear.

That is the space we want KadakKart to own: Indian everyday clothing, made with attitude but without unnecessary noise.

NO LOUD LOGOS.

OUR FIRST CHAPTER

ONE TEE.
DONE RIGHT.

Our first collection starts deliberately small. One core silhouette, built around 100% cotton, 180 GSM and a biowashed finish.

As the brand grows, we want every new product to answer the same question: is this something people will genuinely want to wear?

If the answer is yes, it belongs at KadakKart.
