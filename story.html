THE IDEA

BASIC ISN'T
A BAD WORD.

KadakKart starts with the most ordinary item in a wardrobe: the round-neck T-shirt.

We think “basic” should mean dependable, not forgettable. It should fit into real days — work, travel, weekends, evenings out, lazy Sundays and everything between.

So we are building the brand around the details people actually live with: fabric, weight, softness, fit, repeat wear and value.

NO LOUD LOGOS.

NO PRETENCE.

NO SHORTCUTS.

JUST KADAK.

INDIAN IN MIND

MADE FOR
REAL DAYS.

India has hot summers, humid coastal weather, monsoons, dry inland heat and everything in between. Clothing that looks good in a studio still has to work when you step outside.

That is why our product thinking starts with use, not just aesthetics. Our first tee is a 100% cotton, 180 GSM, biowashed round neck — chosen as a practical everyday balance.

We will keep testing that thinking as the collection grows.

OUR FIRST CHAPTER

ONE TEE.
DONE RIGHT.

We are intentionally starting small. One core silhouette. A limited first drop. A clear material story.

From here, KadakKart can grow into more colours, fits and products — but the rule stays the same: if we would not want to wear it repeatedly, it does not belong here.
